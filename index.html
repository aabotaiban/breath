Sonar Breath

Your iPhone plays an inaudible tone and listens to the echo from your chest. Breathing shifts the echo's phase.

Tap Start. Allow the microphone when Safari asks.
  1. Turn off silent mode and set the volume to about 75%.
  2. Rest the phone 30–50 cm from your chest, bottom edge pointing at you.
  3. Lie or sit still and breathe normally. Give it 15–20 seconds.

Experiment only, not a medical device. Children and pets can hear 18–20 kHz, so keep it away from them.
